# EXP-B0-000: Deterministic fixed-time SUMO substrate and 1X exposure diagnosis

## Status

Completed on 4 September 2026.

- `B0_EMPIRICAL_SUBSTRATE_VALIDATED=YES`
- `B0_CURRENT_DISRUPTION_SCENARIO_SUITABILITY=TOO_WEAK`

The first statement concerns simulator, metric, determinism, and observation
integrity. The second concerns whether the particular 1X demand and disruption
produce enough traffic response for later method comparison. They are not
contradictory.

## Question

Can the smallest suitable multi-intersection network support a deterministic,
auditable fixed-time baseline and directly observable one-lane-loss event
before any learned treatment exists?

## Pretreatment boundary

The run followed the frozen pre-empirical charter and preceded all RL,
capacity-conditioned communication, routing, or dissertation treatment. Only
fixed-time N0 and D0 conditions were measured. The originating repository
revision was `2a0da664c8f43a1e346c3c405fbff3fc40a78778`.

## Metric definitions

- **Restricted mean trip time:** mean over all scheduled trips from scheduled
  departure to arrival, or to `H_PILOT` when unfinished.
- **Restricted P95:** nearest rank at `ceil(0.95 N)` over the same restricted
  trip times.
- **Queue burden:** one-second integral of network-wide active passenger
  vehicles travelling below `0.1 m/s`.
- **Native waiting:** SUMO trip-info waiting time, with complete departed-trip
  coverage and zero contribution for not-yet-departed trips.
- **Throughput:** non-teleported arrivals by `H_PILOT`.
- **Unfinished:** active, not-departed, teleported, failed, or invalid scheduled
  trips at `H_PILOT`.

## Procedure

The frozen runner executed `N0`, `D0`, and exact deterministic repeats `N0-R`
and `D0-R`. The conditions differed only by application of the frozen lane
restriction. Input hashes, TLS behavior, route identity, trip accounting,
collision and teleport events, and permission restoration were checked.

A separate read-only observer diagnostic repeated the same conditions while
recording one-second per-vehicle edge/lane transitions. Its scientific metrics
were reconciled against the original B0 evidence, and the observer was checked
for noninterference.

## Direct exposure diagnostic

Five passenger vehicles directly entered `A1B1` during `[300,600)`. All five
used `A1B1_1`; there were zero post-activation passenger entries onto the
restricted lane `A1B1_0`. Activation and restoration were each observed once,
and the observer did not mutate simulator state.

The matched N0/D0 vehicle diagnostics observed only the required lane-choice
difference. They found no additional A1B1 traversal time, halting, or final
arrival delay for any exposed vehicle. The diagnostic repeated exactly.

## Interpretation

The B0 substrate is empirically validated for deterministic simulation,
measurement, accounting, disruption lifecycle, exposure, and lane compliance.
The 1X scenario is nevertheless `TOO_WEAK`: it changes lane choice without
producing measurable trip-time or queue degradation.

This does not establish RL superiority, graph benefit, capacity-conditioned
communication benefit, the central dissertation hypothesis, generalization,
statistical significance, or publication-level method evidence.

## Decision

Retain `B0_GRID_3X3_V1` and the validated observer. Do not use the 1X event as a
method-comparison scenario. Calibrate the physical scenario using baseline-only
evidence before implementing or evaluating a treatment.
